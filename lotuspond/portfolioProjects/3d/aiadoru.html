Aiadoru

Meet the Mother

Aiadoru is a labor of love vTuber project I started working on in 2024. She is thematically a being from the Liminal Zone that learns how to seem human through extensive observation.

Reference Sheets

The 3D Model

This model was one of the more extensive design challenges I have ever faced, mainly because I had never designed clothes before. I was basically doing the job of an entire team as one person.

The biggest challenge out of the clothing specifically was deciding what tone I was going for. Take a look at the "Logged On" reference sheet. This was one of the original design ideas for Aiadoru that ended taking the back burner because i wanted something a bit more 'regal' of sorts.

After outfit design, however, the process was pretty straight forward. I used vector (shocker!) for the majority of the texture work and played into the skills I already had. With every outfit and iteration of Aiadoru, I tried something a little bit more difficult. Wherein Wanderlust was made using VRoid as a base and texturized, Elegy in Bloom was almost entirely modelled in Blender.

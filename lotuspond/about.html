Hi! I'm Hana.

Let's get to know each other!

Hana (Any Pronouns) is the sole owner and employee behind Lotus Pond Creative. She is a web developer, graphic designer, app developer, 3D modeller, game designer, techical designer as well as a heavy oversharer. She is also terrible at speaking in 3rd person, so she will stop now.

I've been deep into the tech world for the majority of my short life. I started my career making unique websites on Neocities and creating animations in Miku Miku Dance, soon after pivoting to making my own 3D models. Reasonably in 2024, I decided to begin my own design business. This is where I'm at at least 2 years later!

I'm one of those people who wants to be a digital guru of sorts. I've always prided my extensive knowledge of computers and digital products (apps, websites, etc). I may not be able to claim this title yet, but I am always striving towards it! That's the exact reason why there's so many different art forms displayed here. Life is exactly what you make of it, and mine will be made of all kinds of digital art!

The other half of being a guru is inspiring other people. As such, I strive to inspire people like me to explore the world of digital art, as well as to express themselves as authentically as possible, no matter where that might be.
Whether you love maximalist design (like me!) or minimalism, always strive to express your best self! The first impression people get of you offline is your style! Who's to say it's any different online?
